## Welcome to FlixCapacitor

FlixCapacitor is a high-performance streaming application for Android that lets you watch movies, TV shows, and anime. With offline-first design and optional cloud sync, your content and preferences are always available.

### Key Features

- 🎬 **Browse Movies & TV Shows**: Extensive library of content
- 📺 **Anime Support**: Dedicated anime section
- ⭐ **Favorites**: Save your favorite content
- 📚 **Personal Library**: Manage local video files
- 📋 **Watchlist**: Track what to watch next
- 🎥 **Playback Queue**: Queue up episodes and movies
- ☁️ **Cloud Sync**: Sync favorites and settings across devices (optional)
- 📴 **Offline Mode**: Works without internet connection
- 🎨 **Dark Mode**: Easy on the eyes
- ⚡ **Fast & Lightweight**: Optimized for performance

## Watching Videos

### Starting Playback

**From Movie/Show Details:**

1. Tap **Play** button
2. Choose quality (if multiple options available)
3. Video begins loading
4. Playback starts when buffered

**From Favorites/Queue:**

1. Tap favorite or queue item
2. Playback starts automatically

### Video Player Controls

**Basic Controls:**

- **Play/Pause** ▶️⏸️ - Tap center or play button
- **Seek Bar** - Drag to skip forward/backward
- **Volume** 🔊 - Volume slider
- **Fullscreen** ⛶ - Toggle fullscreen mode
- **Back** ← - Exit player

**Advanced Controls:**

- **Settings Gear** ⚙️ - Quality, subtitles, playback speed
- **Subtitle Button** 💬 - Enable/disable subtitles
- **Queue Button** 📋 - View playback queue
- **Cast Button** 📡 - Cast to TV (if supported)

### Quality Settings

1. Tap **Settings Gear** during playback
2. Select **Quality**
3. Choose resolution:
   - **Auto**: Automatic quality adjustment
   - **1080p**: Full HD (high bandwidth)
   - **720p**: HD (moderate bandwidth)
   - **480p**: SD (low bandwidth)
   - **360p**: Low quality (very low bandwidth)

### Subtitles

1. Tap **Subtitle Button** 💬 during playback
2. Select language (if available)
3. Subtitles appear at bottom of screen
4. Tap again to disable

**Subtitle Settings:**

- Font size adjustment (in Settings)
- Background opacity
- Text color (future feature)

### Playback Speed

1. Tap **Settings Gear** during playback
2. Select **Speed**
3. Choose speed:
   - **0.5x** - Slow motion
   - **0.75x** - Slightly slower
   - **1.0x** - Normal speed
   - **1.25x** - Slightly faster
   - **1.5x** - Fast
   - **2.0x** - Double speed

### Picture-in-Picture (PiP)

1. Start video playback
2. Press **Home Button** on device
3. Video continues in small window
4. Tap window to return to full screen
5. Swipe to dismiss

**Note:** PiP requires Android 8.0+ and must be enabled in Settings.

## Playback Queue

### What is Playback Queue?

Queue up multiple videos to watch in sequence. Great for binge-watching TV shows!

### Adding to Queue

**From Details Page:**

1. Open movie/episode details
2. Tap **Add to Queue** button
3. Toast notification: "Added to queue"

**Auto-Queue (TV Shows):**

- Enable **Autoplay Next Episode** in Settings
- After episode ends, next episode auto-adds to queue and plays

### Viewing Queue

1. During playback, tap **Queue Button** 📋
2. Or tap **Menu** → **Playback Queue**
3. View all queued items

### Managing Queue

**Reorder Items:**

1. Tap and hold queue item
2. Drag to new position
3. Release to drop

**Remove from Queue:**

1. Swipe left on queue item
2. Tap **Delete**

**Clear Queue:**

1. Tap **Menu** in queue view
2. Select **Clear All**
3. Confirm

### Queue Behavior

- **Autoplay**: Next item plays automatically after current finishes
- **Loop**: Repeat queue when all items complete (optional in Settings)
- **Shuffle**: Random playback order (optional in Settings)
- **Resume**: Queue persists across app restarts

## Cloud Sync (Optional)

### What is Cloud Sync?

Cloud Sync backs up your favorites and settings to the cloud, enabling cross-device synchronization.

**Note:** Cloud Sync is optional. App works fully offline without an account.

### Creating an Account

1. Tap **Settings** → **Cloud Account & Sync**
2. Tap **Sign In / Sign Up**
3. Enter email and password
4. Tap **Sign Up**
5. Account created!

### Signing In

1. Tap **Settings** → **Cloud Account & Sync**
2. Tap **Sign In**
3. Enter email and password
4. Tap **Sign In**
5. Favorites and settings sync automatically

### Syncing Favorites

**Manual Sync:**

1. Tap **Settings** → **Cloud Account & Sync**
2. Tap **Sync Favorites**
3. Toast notification: "Favorites synced"

**Automatic Sync:**

- Favorites sync automatically when you add/remove items
- Requires active internet connection
- Background sync occurs every hour (when online)

### Syncing Settings

**Manual Sync:**

1. Tap **Settings** → **Cloud Account & Sync**
2. Tap **Sync Settings**
3. Toast notification: "Settings synced"

**Automatic Sync:**

- Settings sync automatically when you change them
- Requires active internet connection

### Restoring from Cloud

**First Time on New Device:**

1. Sign in with your account
2. Favorites and settings sync automatically

**Manual Restore:**

1. Tap **Settings** → **Cloud Account & Sync**
2. Tap **Restore from Cloud**
3. Confirm restore
4. Local data replaced with cloud data

### Signing Out

1. Tap **Settings** → **Cloud Account & Sync**
2. Tap **Sign Out**
3. Confirm sign out
4. Local favorites and settings remain on device

## Troubleshooting

### App Won't Open

**Solution:**

1. Force close app: Settings → Apps → FlixCapacitor → Force Stop
2. Clear app cache: Settings → Apps → FlixCapacitor → Clear Cache
3. Restart device
4. Reinstall app if issue persists

### Video Won't Play

**Solutions:**

1. **Check Internet**: Ensure device is connected to internet
2. **Try Different Quality**: Lower quality may work better
3. **Clear Cache**: Settings → Performance → Clear Cache
4. **Restart App**: Force close and reopen
5. **Try Different Content**: Issue may be with specific video

### Video Keeps Buffering

**Solutions:**

1. **Lower Quality**: Settings → Streaming → Quality Preference → 480p
2. **Check Internet Speed**: Ensure stable connection (3+ Mbps recommended)
3. **Pause and Wait**: Let video buffer before playing
4. **Wi-Fi Instead of Cellular**: More stable connection
5. **Clear Cache**: Free up space for buffering

### Subtitles Not Showing

**Solutions:**

1. **Enable Subtitles**: Tap subtitle button during playback
2. **Check Availability**: Not all content has subtitles
3. **Change Language**: Try different subtitle language
4. **Restart Playback**: Exit and re-enter video
5. **Update App**: Ensure you have latest version

### Favorites Not Syncing

**Solutions:**

1. **Check Internet**: Ensure device is online
2. **Sign In**: Settings → Cloud Account & Sync → Sign In
3. **Manual Sync**: Tap "Sync Favorites" button
4. **Check Account**: Ensure correct account is signed in
5. **Wait**: Sync may take a few moments

### App Crashes

**Solutions:**

1. **Update App**: Install latest version
2. **Clear Data**: Settings → Apps → FlixCapacitor → Clear Data (resets app)
3. **Free Storage**: Ensure device has 500MB+ free space
4. **Reinstall App**: Uninstall and reinstall
5. **Report Bug**: Contact support with crash details

### Playback Queue Not Working

**Solutions:**

1. **Check Queue**: Menu → Playback Queue (ensure items are added)
2. **Enable Autoplay**: Settings → Streaming → Autoplay Next Episode
3. **Clear Queue**: Menu → Playback Queue → Clear All, then re-add
4. **Restart App**: Force close and reopen

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is FlixCapacitor free?**
A: Yes, FlixCapacitor is completely free to use.

**Q: Do I need an account?**
A: No, accounts are optional. Cloud sync requires an account, but all other features work without one.

**Q: Does FlixCapacitor work offline?**
A: Yes, you can browse cached content, access favorites, play local videos, and use settings offline. Streaming requires internet.

**Q: What Android version is required?**
A: Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher is recommended. Android 6.0+ may work with limited features.

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes, all data is stored locally on your device. Cloud sync (if enabled) uses secure encryption.

### Content Questions

**Q: Where does FlixCapacitor get content?**
A: Content comes from curated APIs and public domain sources. All content is freely available online.

**Q: Can I request specific movies/shows?**
A: Content availability depends on sources. We don't control what content is available.

**Q: Why is some content missing details?**
A: Metadata (posters, plots, ratings) comes from third-party APIs. Some content may have incomplete information.

**Q: Are subtitles available?**
A: Subtitles are available for content that includes them. Availability varies by content.

### Technical Questions

**Q: How much data does streaming use?**
A: Data usage depends on quality:
- 360p: ~300MB/hour
- 480p: ~700MB/hour
- 720p: ~1.5GB/hour
- 1080p: ~3GB/hour

**Q: How much storage does the app need?**
A: App is ~50MB. Cache can grow to 500MB-5GB depending on settings.

**Q: Can I cast to my TV?**
A: Casting support is planned for future release.

**Q: Does FlixCapacitor support Android TV?**
A: Android TV support is planned for future release. App may work on Android TV but is not optimized for it.

### Privacy & Security Questions

**Q: What data does FlixCapacitor collect?**
A: By default, no personal data is collected. Anonymous usage analytics are optional (opt-in).

**Q: Are my favorites private?**
A: Favorites are stored locally on your device. Cloud sync (if enabled) stores encrypted favorites in your personal account.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Yes, sign in and go to Settings → Cloud Account & Sync → Delete Account.

**Q: Is my payment information safe?**
A: FlixCapacitor is free and does not collect payment information.

### Troubleshooting Questions

**Q: Why can't I find a specific movie?**
A: Search availability depends on content sources. Try searching by partial title or alternative spellings.

**Q: Why is playback choppy?**
A: Try lowering video quality, closing background apps, or ensuring stable internet connection.

**Q: Why won't the app install?**
A: Ensure "Unknown Sources" is enabled in device security settings. Check that device has Android 6.0+.

**Q: How do I report a bug?**
A: Open GitHub Issues at https://github.com/flixcapacitor/popcorn-mobile/issues
